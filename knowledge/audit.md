**Root cause:** accidental duplication across cloned projects, plus optimizing to incomplete known-fault proxy labels rather than the withheld new-fault population. Equal four-digit scores *alone* are not proof of identical files, but identical independently retrieved SHA-256 hashes are proof of identical committed bytes. The current candidate SHA-256 `ae3ec2c5942f44d78b8b0d77539733b987549cddf78226bcb8071e03627c4ebf` differs, but novelty is **not** skill: it failed the holdout.

**Irregularity to review:** the public site of 8GEMSDOE says `Hedge-v2` is pixel-identical to another artifact and also claims a 0.1563 floor on an *unuploaded* file. The masked-known-fault rule is [staff-confirmed](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/2), but a public-score floor for an unuploaded union depends on whether the known-pixel predictions affect TP near new ground truth; do not treat that floor as an observed leaderboard score. The public page is not primary evidence of upload→score mapping.
