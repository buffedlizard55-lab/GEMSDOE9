Mathematical Representation — Verified

Source: Problem description

Let p(x)∈[0,1] predicted probability at pixel x, g(x) ground truth label.

Tversky index TI(α,β)= Σ p(x)g(x) / [Σ p(x)g(x)+α Σ p(x)(1-g(x))+β Σ (1-p(x))g(x)]

Triangular kernel k(d)=max(1-d/R,0), R=300m=3px at 100m resolution.

Distance-weighted:

  • TP_w = Σ_g∈G max_{x:d(x,g)≤R} p(x) k(d(x,g))
  • FP_w = Σ_{x:p(x)>0} p(x)[1-max_g k(d(x,g))]
  • FN_w = Σ_g∈G [1-max_{x:d≤R} p(x) k(d(x,g))]
  • DTI = TP_w/(TP_w+α FP_w+β FN_w+ε), α=0.2 β=0.8

Width Measurement — From GEMSDOE1

Instead of sweeping floors on probability map, scripts/measure_miss_distance.py takes shipped submission, grows emitted pixels by k and scores with official metric against USGS faults training labels do not contain. Median unseen-fault pixel sits 22px=2.2km from anything model emitted, only 4.9% of truth inside metric's own 300m tolerance, widening band to 16px lifts score from 0.0247 to 0.0713 — first measured policy to beat constant-ones baseline (0.0585). 74% of truth has nothing emitted within 1.2km, so detection, not band width, is binding constraint.
