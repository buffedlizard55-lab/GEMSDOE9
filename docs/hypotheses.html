H9-1: Relay Stepover & Intersection Density (Top Candidate) — Rank 1

Expected lift: +0.06-0.09 over 0.1563 baseline → 0.22-0.25 DTI, Cost: Low-Med (derive from existing 19 bands, no external data)

  • Specific layer(s): Dilatation rate (band 4), shear strain rate (band 5), second invariant (band 6) + detrended elevation slope (band 3) + isostatic gravity slope (band 8) + derived fault azimuth from structure-tensor coherence (new, from detrended elevation)
  • Physical signature: Extensional relay stepovers (left-stepping in right-lateral Walker Lane) and fault intersections create dilational jogs where permeability spikes 10-100×. Signature = high dilation tendency Td × intersection density (Hough line intersections per km²) × curvature inflection. Target 2-5 km stepovers. Td = (σ1-σn)/(σ1-σ3) ≈ 0.5*(1+dilatation/(shear+|second|)). Intersection density via gradient of orientation.
  • Why catches missing: USGS catalogue maps through-going traces, but misses short linking faults inside stepovers that have subtle topographic expression but strong strain signal. Geothermal upflow at Steamboat, Brady, Dixie Valley is at stepovers, not mid-segment. 70% of Great Basin geothermal at stepovers (Faulds et al. 2010, verified).
  • How differs from siblings: GEMSDOE4 used lineament features (Sato ridgeness, structure-tensor coherence) but not explicit stepover detection or dilation tendency. 6GEMSDOE used 88 channels (horizontal-gradient magnitude, analytic-signal, tilt, curvature, break-in-slope, structure-tensor) but no Td or intersection density. 8GEMSDOE used scarp + radiometric but not strain-derived Td. First to compute Td from strain rate tensor and cross with Hough intersections.
  • Implementation: src/gems/features.py: dilation_tendency(), intersection_density(), build_h91_features() — derived from existing bands, no new external data. Free official sources: strain rate bands from training_features.tif (verified).
  • Validation: Should beat 0.0895 structural-coherence on Fold0 at 3% budget → projected 0.095-0.11. If >0.0895, allowed to spend weekly slot.

H9-2: Conductivity Edge & Clay Cap Gradient — Rank 2

Expected lift: +0.04-0.07 → 0.20-0.23, Cost: Low (derive from existing)

  • Layers: Surface conductivity (band 0), depth to conductive base (band 1) + isostatic gravity (band 7) + radiometric Th/K (clay proxy, band from GeoDAWN 99.975% coverage)
  • Signature: Blind geothermal = conductive smectite clay cap (1-10 Ωm) over resistive reservoir, with sharp lateral edge at fault. Signature = Laplacian of conductivity ∇²(cond) + vertical gradient cond/depth + horizontal gradient magnitude HGM. Edge detection via zero-crossing of Laplacian.
  • Why missing: USGS maps faults from surface scarp, but blind faults have no scarp — only conductivity edge from clay alteration. INGENIOUS includes some blind but not all. Targets blind.
  • Differs: Previous used conductivity as raw band, not edge. 6GEMSDOE used analytic signal but not conductivity Laplacian. First to use clay cap model from Cumming 2009.
  • Source: Conductivity bands from training_features.tif, free official. No external data needed.

H9-3: Fluvial Geomorphic Anomaly (SL Index & ksn) — Rank 3

Expected lift: +0.03-0.06 → 0.19-0.22, Cost: Medium (requires 10m DEM download ~5GB + processing)

  • Layers: External 10m 3DEP DEM (free official https://apps.nationalmap.gov/3dep/) derived: slope, curvature, SL index (stream length-gradient), normalized channel steepness ksn, drainage deflection angle
  • Signature: Faults perturb longitudinal river profiles → knickpoints, high SL, low ksn upstream. Signature = SL anomaly >2σ + ksn drop + channel 90° deflection. Targets faults with no scarp but with drainage offset.
  • Why missing: USGS faults mapped from scarp and aerial photos, misses blind faults that only show in drainage. Walker Lane has many such (e.g., Dixie Valley).
  • Differs: GEMSDOE used detrended elevation slope and break-in-slope, but not fluvial indices. 8GEMSDOE used 10m scarp but not SL/ksn. New external DEM processing via whitebox or richdem.
  • Source needed: USGS 3DEP 10m DEM — free official, 100% coverage, verified in 8GEMSDOE as 9 topo/scarp bands. Obtainable: https://apps.nationalmap.gov/3dep/ — check live, yes obtainable. Implementation cost medium.

H9-4: Thermal & Alteration Halo (Landsat TIRS + ASTER) — Rank 4

Expected lift: +0.05-0.10 → 0.21-0.26 (higher variance), Cost: Medium-High (external download, cloud masking, topo correction)

  • Layers: External Landsat-8/9 Band10-11 thermal (100m resampled) + ASTER alteration indices: kaolinite (B5+B7)/B6, alunite B3/B5, clay (B5*B7)/B6² — free official via USGS EarthExplorer https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/ and NASA Earthdata https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/
  • Signature: Geothermal = 2-5K thermal anomaly + clay alteration halo (kaolinite, alunite, silica) 100-500m wide. Signature = thermal anomaly >1.5σ after topo correction + clay index > threshold + proximity to conductivity edge.
  • Why missing: Catalogue faults have no thermal requirement; hidden geothermal faults have alteration but no scarp. Directly targets geothermal permeability, not just fault existence.
  • Differs: No previous repo used thermal or ASTER. Completely new data source.
  • Source needed: Landsat-8/9 TIRS and ASTER L1T — free official, obtainable via EarthExplorer (verified live). Cost medium-high due to cloud masking and topo correction.

H9-5: Magnetotelluric Dimensionality & Euler Depth Contact — Rank 5

Expected lift: +0.02-0.05 → 0.18-0.21, Cost: Low-Med (derived from existing bands, compute-heavy)

  • Layers: Reduced-to-pole magnetic anomaly (band 9), TMI (10), vertical/horizontal slope (11,12), top-of-crustal magnetic source depth (13) + isostatic gravity anomaly (7) + slope (8)
  • Signature: Faults = contact between different magnetic susceptibilities → high horizontal gradient magnitude + analytic signal + tilt derivative zero-crossing + Euler deconvolution depth <1km. Signature = (HGM >75th percentile) ∧ (tilt zero) ∧ (Euler depth <1km) ∧ (gravity HGM coincident).
  • Why missing: USGS catalogue includes faults with clear magnetic lineament, but misses those with subtle contact where Euler depth indicates shallow source but no surface scarp. Targets basement-involved faults.
  • Differs: 6GEMSDOE used tilt derivative and analytic signal amplitude as features for HGB, but not Euler deconvolution or joint gravity-magnetic coincidence. First to compute Euler depth and enforce gravity-magnetic coincidence.
  • Source: Mag & grav bands from training_features.tif, free official, no external data.
